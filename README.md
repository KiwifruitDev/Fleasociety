# Fleasociety

```
fleasociety:
you're the inventor of a miracle flea prevention cream in a world full of anthros (taking place around the birth of science)
it's a time management game where you create new personalized solutions of cream and serve customers of different species by applying this cream onto their fur and plucking out fleas
at the end of the day you're scored by newspaper critics and every few days you get new upgrades and different kinds of customers
it is like a papa's pizzeria-type game
```
